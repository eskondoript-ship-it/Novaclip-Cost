Can one beast replace all of them

No, and it is worth knowing why. Fourteen machines is twenty-eight cards. The 9950X is a consumer chip with 24 PCIe lanes, so it takes two cards and that is the end of it. To go past that you need Threadripper PRO or EPYC and 128 lanes, which tops out around four to eight cards in one box — not twenty-eight. One real beast would be four RTX 6000 Blackwell at roughly €8,000 each, so €40,000 of cards before anything else, and it still would not do the work of fourteen.

The wall is electricity, not money. Fourteen of your machines average 5.6 kW, around the clock. A Portuguese home supply is usually 3.45 to 6.9 kW in total. You would be running the whole house at its limit, forever, for €10,800 of electricity a year. A single beast of four big cards plus its CPU pulls 2 to 3 kW on its own and needs two power supplies, proper wiring and somewhere that is not a bedroom.

So you do not build the beast. You need fewer free users per customer, or fewer requests each. These three cost nothing in hardware:

What this is built on

Free users and customers are not the same thing. A free user has signed up and pays nothing. A customer pays. Out of every 100 free users only a couple ever become customers — but the AI bill arrives for all of them, which is why the free crowd is the cost and the customers are the income.

Facts: plan prices from NovaClip's own pricing page (€6.99 / €4.99 / €9.99, certificates €24.99–€99.99), Stripe at 2.2% + €0.25, AI at €2.30 a year for every free user and customer from the costs document, and the three-way AI comparison in it.

Guesses: conversion, churn and the certificate rate. None has been measured, because the payment links are not live yet. They are also the three numbers that move this page most — which is the argument for measuring them before spending €20,000 on ads.

What the odds do and do not say: they are the chance of making a profit once you already have that many customers. They are not the chance of getting there. Reaching 500 is the harder half, and that is what the ad budget is for.

How profit is worked out: you only bill 11.2 months a year, because each year you replace whoever left and every replacement gets the free months. Certificates carry no AI cost, so almost all of that money is profit. Everything else carries the 12% spare from the costs document.
